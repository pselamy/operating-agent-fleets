## Reading conventions

- **Verified** means checked against a public system of record or an approved confidential source at the stated cutoff.
- **Internally corroborated; not publicly reproducible** means a reviewer verified restricted evidence without publishing its locator or contents.
- **Proposed** means desired design, not confirmed current behavior.
- **Environment-specific** identifies a local implementation choice rather than a transferable principle.

No chapter may cite an artifact as evidence before that artifact has a valid public lineage record.
